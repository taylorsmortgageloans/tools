What common listing incentives actually cost the seller — and how to figure it yourself

4How to calculate it yourself

Step 1 — Monthly payment at any rate
Payment = L × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1)
where L = loan amount, r = annual rate ÷ 12 (as a decimal), n = 360 (months for a 30-yr loan).
Step 2 — Cost of a buydown
For each reduced year, take (full payment − reduced payment) × 12, then add the years together.
• 1-0: Yr1 only (rate −1%)
• 2-1: Yr1 (rate −2%) + Yr2 (rate −1%)
• 3-2-1: Yr1 (rate −3%) + Yr2 (rate −2%) + Yr3 (rate −1%)
Step 3 — Cost of "6 months of interest"
Quick estimate: Loan × annual rate ÷ 2.  (e.g. $482,500 × 6.5% ÷ 2 ≈ $15,681)
The buyer's payment during those 6 months drops to just the principal portion.

5Other incentives & how they compare

Flat closing-cost credit

A $10,000 credit costs the seller exactly $10,000. It's simple and flexible — but it does not lower the buyer's monthly payment the way a buydown does. Same dollars, very different "feel" for the buyer.

Price reduction vs. buydown

Dropping the price $10,000 (on a $482,500 loan @ 6.5%) lowers the payment only ~$63/mo — permanently. That same ~$11K spent on a 2-1 buydown cuts the payment ~$605/mo in year one. Buydowns buy far bigger early relief per dollar; price cuts help long-term equity.

6Talking points

  • Lead with the monthly payment, not the total. "$21K sounds like a lot — but it drops your buyer's payment nearly $900 a month for the first year" lands better than the lump sum.
  • Buydowns beat price cuts for affordability. Dollar for dollar, a buydown gives a buyer far more monthly relief up front than an equivalent price reduction.
  • Unused buydown funds aren't lost. If the buyer refinances or sells during the buydown period, remaining escrow is typically credited to the loan.
  • Match the incentive to the buyer. Payment-sensitive buyers → buydown or 6-months interest. Cash-tight buyers → closing-cost credit.
  • Always confirm final numbers with the lender. These are estimates for conversation; the lender's figures govern.
