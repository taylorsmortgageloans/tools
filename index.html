For real estate professional use in structuring offers. Figures are estimates for illustration only and are not a loan approval, commitment to lend, or offer to extend credit. Payments shown cover principal and interest; property taxes, insurance, monthly mortgage insurance, and HOA dues are separate and remain the buyer's responsibility during the seller-paid period. Interest is calculated on a standard 30-year amortization schedule and assumes payments are made as scheduled with no additional principal. FHA seller contributions are capped at 6% of the sales price and apply to all seller-paid costs combined, including closing costs and prepaids.
